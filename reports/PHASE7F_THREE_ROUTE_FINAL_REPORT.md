## 6. Honest Geometry Reporting

Because variants share the same risk field, grid, algorithm, speed, and safety threshold,
their A* paths can coincide for a given scenario. The publisher compares the **actual
waypoint arrays** after every solve and prints the truth to stdout:

```
Geometry: all three variants produce identical waypoint arrays for this scenario
Geometry: two variants share an identical waypoint array for this scenario
Geometry: the three variants produce three distinct waypoint arrays
```

The dashboard renders the optimizer output as-is. Identical geometries overlap honestly
(selected variant drawn last, on top). **No value is fabricated or perturbed to force
visual divergence.**

*The runtime geometry identity is printed by the publisher when the pipeline executes —
pre-filled values are intentionally not invented in this report.*
